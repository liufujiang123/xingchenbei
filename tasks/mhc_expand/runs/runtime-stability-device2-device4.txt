MhcExpand host ACL set-device stability test
Date (UTC): 2026-08-19

Scope:
  No MhcExpand, custom OPP, context, stream, tensor, or kernel was loaded or
  created. Every sample was a new /usr/bin/python3 process running the same
  task-local probe with a 30-second timeout:

    aclInit -> aclrtSetDevice -> aclrtResetDevice -> aclFinalize

  aclrtResetDevice here is the normal lifecycle pairing for a successful
  aclrtSetDevice call. No administrative device reset was performed.

INTERPRETATION

  All 16 independent processes completed the requested lifecycle within the
  30-second limit. A 9.6-10.7 second first-run delay occurred on both devices
  and both CANN versions, followed by sub-0.6-second set-device calls. This is
  consistent with a host-wide cold-start cost in this experiment, not a
  device-2-specific timeout.

  The earlier timeout inside the test harness AclRuntime constructor cannot be
  assigned specifically to aclrtSetDevice because that constructor also calls
  aclrtCreateContext and aclrtCreateStream without per-call stage timestamps.
  This set-device-only stability result therefore does not prove that the full
  context/stream lifecycle is stable.

CLASSIFICATION

  RUNTIME STABLE

  This classification applies exactly to the requested four-call minimal
  lifecycle and 30-second threshold.
